# NIVAR G2 — visual assets and provenance

G2 experimental, human selection pending. Production and inspection date: 2026-09-11.

## The owner's directional correction

Invented laboratory instruments, calibration specimens and black cylindrical evidence objects are rejected. Evidence is expressed through energy infrastructure, electrical measurement, native data records, charts, source labels, documents and contradictory examination. The composition and typographic direction continue; the rejected metaphor does not.

Four initial assets were removed from `public/g2` and retained only under `rejected-assets/` as process history: `evidence-specimen.webp`, `hardware-measurement.webp`, `evidence-source-motion.mp4`, and `evidence-instrument.glb`. The editable Blender study and its render are historical rejected work, not product deliverables. `motion/initial-asset-study.md` documents the original production, not the final runtime selection. The three initial generated hero studies under `references/` are likewise exploratory and must not be treated as approval of their objects.

## Product film

The selected hero is an authored 18-second React/CSS/SVG composition in `src/components/g2/HeroFilm.tsx` and `hero-film.css`, inspectable at `/br`. It transforms one evidence record through six chapters. Source, unit, coverage and period are native text, not generated pixels. Its numerical series is explicitly synthetic and is never attributed to ONS, CCEE, the pictured infrastructure or an actual customer. Play/pause, restart, direct chapter selection, reduced-motion and visibility behavior belong to the runtime.

The earlier six-second Seedance object clip was inspected and rejected. It is not used in the final hero. No rendered video file is presented as a completed substitute for the native product-film prototype.

## Geometry, emblems and typography

The Terminal's Brazil geometry uses the existing IBGE-derived repository asset and preserves its source link. It is not generated from an image. Native diagrams and charts are code; the synthetic dataset and reproducible transformations are disclosed in the source drawer and CSV.

The custom geometric NIVAR wordmark is original SVG in `Brand.tsx`. Existing mythic family emblems are reused from the repository's `/patronos/` files without modifying them. Their monochrome treatment is scoped to G2. Alexandria's identity, fonts and imagery remain untouched.

Newsreader, Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted through Fontsource. Their full SIL Open Font License notices ship under `/g2/licenses/`. No third-party logo or stock model is embedded in the new wordmark or charts.

## Inspection and excluded research

Original images and final runtime crops were visually inspected; desktop and mobile screenshots are under `screens/`. Native browser capture replaced an early in-app raster scaling issue. Rejected object studies remain visibly labeled in this record.
