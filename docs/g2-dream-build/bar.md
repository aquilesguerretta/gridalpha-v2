## Seven observable mechanisms

1. **One record keeps its identity while its function changes.** In Subframe, a black token remains recognizably the same object as it becomes a display containing UI and then code. For NIVAR, preserve the source and reference of an energy observation through an infrastructure image, its document or data record, chart, challenge and published reading. The owner's correction excludes invented science instruments, laboratory props and abstract black cylinders. The transferred mechanism is continuity of evidence, never the literal token. A cut to six unrelated scenes fails this mechanism.
2. **The stage stays still while the evidence changes form.** The Subframe object is contained in one neutral square with stable lighting and a consistent camera axis. For NIVAR, keep a stable document margin, chart baseline, map coordinate or source annotation across all six chapters. Avoid camera motion and content motion competing. No repeated laboratory metaphor.
3. **A short statement has a single adjacent visual demonstration.** Subframe pairs each functional chapter with its corresponding object state; Linear places a literal issue and its operational context below its thesis. For NIVAR, each verb gets an object change that communicates the verb even when the caption is covered.
4. **The dominant visual reads before its small details.** Subframe's dark object is isolated against a light field. Linear separates its large thesis, supporting sentence, and dense product by scale and vertical space. For NIVAR, a screenshot reduced to 25% should still have one dominant object and one headline, with metadata subordinate.
5. **Identity survives with almost no color.** Both captures primarily use light and dark values; color in Linear's product is attached to states. For NIVAR, no chapter may depend on a full-frame accent wash. Use an accent only for selection, contradiction, or the current signal.
6. **The product is inspectable as a product.** Linear's vignette has readable issue title, context, activity, and fixed property area rather than a decorative laptop. NIVAR's product passage must include legible source, unit, period, and a real workflow or chart relation. Tiny ornamental UI texture fails.
7. **Mobile has a deliberate, self-contained composition.** Subframe's mobile capture keeps the complete object and next explanatory sentence within the same reading sequence. NIVAR's 390px hero must preserve the evidence object and active verb without horizontal scrolling, clipped type, or a desktop composition scaled down.

## Critic instructions

Split the hero into: (1) opening and measurement; (2) structure, observation, and contradiction; (3) transmission and return to inquiry. Brief critic receives the user goal and rendered frames only. System critic receives `design-system.md` and rendered frames only. Craft critic receives this file, reference PNGs/live reference, and unlabeled rendered comparison frames, without builder rationale. Verdict is PASS or FAIL, naming one largest gap. A still-only critic cannot certify temporal continuity or loop timing; flag that blindness explicitly until it sees a frame sequence or playback.

The target cadence remains the user-approved 15–18 second silent loop. This duration is a NIVAR brief requirement, not a timing measured from the Subframe reference. Test reduced motion, pause/replay controls, first-frame completeness, and loop seam separately.

## Rejected literal transfers

Do not copy Subframe's retro terminal, its phone mockup, slogan, rounded gray card, or brand. Do not copy Linear's typeface, navigation, dark identity, or exact app geometry. Preserve mechanisms and let NIVAR's evidence, mineral material, typography roles, and independence determine the visible result.
