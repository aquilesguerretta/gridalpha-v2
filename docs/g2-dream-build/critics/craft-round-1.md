## Binary piece verdicts

### 1. Opening and measurement — PASS

Frames 1 on desktop and mobile show a coherent infrastructure scene with a foreground numerical record. The large 68 and adjacent MW make measurement understandable without the caption. The dark landscape and pale record establish a clear value hierarchy; the restrained accents are not carrying the composition. On desktop, the main thesis reads before the diagram. On mobile, the complete evidence stage and active statement fit together in a self-contained reading sequence rather than reproducing the desktop columns at a smaller scale.

This passes the visual composition bar. It does not certify the truth of the illustrative record, temporal continuity, or accessibility of the controls.

### 2. Structure, observation, and contradiction — FAIL

**Single largest observable gap: the chart relation is too small to inspect at the size at which it is presented.** Frame 2's table is understandable, but frames 3–4 shrink the chart ticks, missing-data legend, and evidence metadata into fine print. This is most acute at 390px, where the plot occupies only roughly 200px of horizontal space after its legend. The change from observation to contradiction is principally two small rings and question marks. At reduced viewing size the same rising line dominates both chapters; the missing observations do not challenge that first impression strongly enough. The adjacent prose explains the doubt more effectively than the visual demonstrates it.

**Concrete improvement:** give the plot the dominant area of the middle chapters, reduce the repeated landscape to a source thumbnail, and place source, unit, and period in one comfortably readable row. Make the two absent intervals clearly open breaks or substantial marked regions in the plot, with a short directly attached label. The absence must remain the most obvious change when the adjacent caption is covered. Keep EV-001 and the plot baseline fixed so this enlargement strengthens the existing continuity.

### 3. Transmission and return to inquiry — FAIL

**Single largest observable gap: the return substitutes an identity card for the evidence action.** Frame 5 has a recognizably published reading with a limitation attached. In frame 6, the record collapses to an almost empty EV-001 strip and the large wordmark and motto become the dominant object. The landscape and source strip persist, but the measurement, uncertainty, and next question do not. Cover the caption and the image reads as a closing brand slate; it does not demonstrate returning to inquiry.

**Concrete improvement:** retain the published reading in its established record position, expose its unresolved coverage question as the final prominent line, and connect that question back to the source thumbnail or the two missing intervals. Return the same visible record to its measurement state for the next cycle. Make the identity mark subordinate. A reader should be able to follow what remains unknown without relying on the words below the stage.
