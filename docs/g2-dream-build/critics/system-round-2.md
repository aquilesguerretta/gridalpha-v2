## System observations

- **One evidence record:** `EV–001`, active power in MW, the illustrative 00–20 h period, and the recurring synthetic-source strip establish continuity even when the document changes from measurement to chart to note. The final six-interval table reconciles the earlier chart and its missing coverage.
- **Actual rendered hierarchy:** The desktop house headline appears near the canon’s 68–80px scale. Inside the evidence stage, numerical values are substantially larger than the approximately 11–12px captions; interpretations are approximately 24–34px, functional values/prose approximately 13–14px, and mono is confined to short identifiers, units, times, and labels. Those internal sizes read as instrument/document hierarchy rather than page-section headings. Source captions are small but decipherable at the native screenshot sizes and do not rely on pale text over busy imagery.
- **Palette roles:** The mineral public canvas, ferro ink, thin rules, and plain document insets remain restrained. Lavender marks the selected chapter and hypothetical analytical relation. Oxide accompanies the revised conclusion and openness to new evidence. The dark chart uses amber for absent-data attention, with written labels preserving meaning independently of color. There is no accent-color page wash or general CTA-color drift visible.
- **Mobile composition:** The 390px views use approximately 20px outer margins, stack content vertically, and retain the decision-useful source, unit, period, interpretation, and chart/table values. The six chapters recompose into two rows of three. The mobile opening gives the instrument priority; it is a deliberate alternate composition rather than a scaled-down desktop split.
- **Visible controls:** All six chapter names remain visible, with an underline distinguishing the selected chapter; play and restart glyphs and elapsed/total duration remain visible in every supplied view. Their surrounding spacing appears sufficient to distinguish the controls. The native screenshots alone cannot establish actual touch-target dimensions, accessible names, keyboard behavior, or focus visibility. The mobile menu icon is visible; no expanded-menu state was supplied.

No blocking visual design-system gap is evident in these three pieces at the supplied resolutions. This is visual approval of the supplied stills only.
