Argos, beyond the small icon.

NIVAR G2.1 / TWO MATERIAL DIRECTIONS
01 / SHALLOW RELIEF / REJECTED DIRECTION

A well-made object. The wrong register.

The relief has controlled texture and strong physical presence, but the circular disk, metallic body and costume produce a Greco-Roman coin. Its material overstates the mythology and can turn the family into a mascot.

02 / INTAGLIO / RECOMMENDED DIRECTION

Character as an editorial instrument.

A cropped profile and observing hand retain Argos. Lavender-black hatching and tactile mineral paper give depth; the copper observation line carries the action into the page. The costume and medallion disappear, leaving gesture and interpretation.
