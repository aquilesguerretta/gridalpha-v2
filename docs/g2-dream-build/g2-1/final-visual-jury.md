# Final visual jury

Independent assessment of the supplied screenshots only. No source, brief, rationale, other documents, or test reports were inspected. These are still-image judgments; they establish nothing about motion or interaction quality.

| Surface | Does its current visual craft compete with the strongest supplied references? | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public NIVAR pages | Yes | The display typography, asymmetric editorial composition, image scale, and restrained identity form a credible premium visual language. The work can stand beside the references without imitating their aesthetic. |
| Terminal | No | It is a handsome, clearly organized product with a distinct identity, but its main surfaces remain too uniformly flat to match the strongest reference's depth and optical finish. |

**Public pages: YES, with a visible material weakness.** The desktop portal's oversized serif headline and compact right-hand introduction create a confident composition. The transition into the full-width landscape is decisive, and the fine annotation line connects the subject to the reading card. On mobile, the headline remains expressive without collapsing the page's hierarchy. The Intelligence desktop screen is especially successful: the enormous title, small engraving, dam photograph, and overlapping warm paper create several scales of attention without losing order. Advisory's large image and offset evidence sheet also feel deliberately composed.

The result is competitive through editorial precision. Media.Work's sharp specular reflections, Rivian's strongly staged subject and light, and DGC's saturated photographic rhythm each deliver more immediate sensory intensity. NIVAR's quieter composition is nevertheless comparably authored. Its serif, sans, and small monospaced labels have purposeful roles; the warm paper, dark instruments, engravings, and amber markers make the identity recognizable across the inspected pages.

The qualification is that this quality is uneven at the material level. The desktop Intelligence paper has the best tactile presence. The portal's gray reading card is much more like a gradient rectangle floating above a photograph. The mobile Intelligence blend between engraving and landscape is attractive but softens both images at the join. The mobile Software screen's thick pale frame around the Terminal feels more mechanically inserted than the other overlaps. These are real weaknesses, but they do not erase the public pages' competitive composition and typography.

**Terminal: NO. Its single largest remaining gap is the depth hierarchy of the main instrument surfaces.** The desktop three-column composition is strong: geography on the left, a dominant numerical/chart field in the middle, and a readable interpretation on the right. The amber selection connects the map, line, chosen observation, and note with economy. The serif interpretation headline also gives the product a voice that generic dashboards often lack. The mobile chart preserves a large current value and useful space for the plotted line; its observed point remains easy to find.

The broad center panel, plot rectangle, annotation strip, controls, and summary mostly read as adjacent blue-gray fills. Their large differences in function are expressed through repeated borders and rectangular bands more than through carefully differentiated optical depth. The selected-observation tooltip is the strongest exception: its bright edge, slight tonal variation, and shadow establish a clear foreground. That local success makes the rest of the instrument look flatter by comparison. Numerous small mono labels and rules also have similar visual weight, adding uniform texture where the eye would benefit from more selective quiet.

The Dovetail reference makes panel boundaries and the floating conversation layer readily legible through restrained border contrast and shadow. Fey is the stronger material benchmark: its layered dark cards have finer edge light, subtle tonal variation, and a more coherent relationship between the surrounding black field and the foreground. Its promotional crop and dark fade conceal information, so those effects should not be treated as requirements for a working Terminal. Even allowing for that difference, NIVAR's principal panel stack has not yet reached the same surface refinement. This verdict does not ask for lower readability, a device mockup, or promotional staging; the gap is visible in the surfaces themselves.

**Weakest material gap across NIVAR: convincing glass/translucency.** In the material-lab screenshot, the pale central glass card reads predominantly as an opaque lilac slab; the landscape does not visibly explain its interior surface. The portal card is more atmospheric, but its broad gray gradient similarly carries most of the effect. By contrast, the warm paper and the Terminal's observed-point tooltip already communicate their intended material and position more convincingly. The highest-value refinement would be a clearer, restrained relationship among background light, panel edge, interior tone, and foreground elevation. More decorative effects would not resolve that relationship by themselves.

## Final Terminal re-review — 2026-09-11, 20:15 UTC

**Current Terminal verdict: YES. Its current visual craft can compete with the supplied Dovetail and Fey benchmarks.** The earlier NO above remains the assessment of the earlier screenshots. This updated verdict is based only on the new rendered images listed below and on reopening the two benchmark images; no implementation rationale or product source was consulted.

The particular weakness behind the earlier NO has materially changed. In the new desktop standalone render, the main instrument has a subtly lit upper surface, the chart sits in a darker recessed field, and the observation card sits distinctly above it. Its bright fine edge, soft shadow, and visibly softened continuation of the chart line establish translucency rather than merely suggesting it through a gradient. The smaller controls and statistics recede appropriately. These are readable differences of depth across the instrument, not simply a replacement of blue-gray with another hue.

The restrained charcoal/plum palette also connects the Terminal more naturally to the public house's pale, slightly violet ground and warm accents. In the desktop House image, the embedded product has presence without becoming a disconnected blue block. The mobile House image keeps that relationship at a smaller scale. On the standalone phone chart, the large value, amber line, selected point, and lifted observation card still form an immediate hierarchy; the material treatment survives the narrower frame.

The paper render provides useful corroborating evidence. Its main reading surface, inset plot, and raised observation card remain distinct in light tones. The phone House paper view also separates the instrument from the surrounding page with restrained shadow. It is more convincingly a set of layered reading surfaces than the former sequence of similarly weighted bands.

Against Dovetail, NIVAR now has a comparably deliberate relationship among its dark base, content surfaces, and foreground observation, with a stronger individual editorial voice in the accompanying text panel. Fey still has finer atmospheric texture and edge illumination, and its promotional composition is more theatrical. NIVAR does not equal every aspect of that finish. However, the latest screenshots present a credible premium instrument whose material hierarchy is now coherent alongside its already strong composition and typography. The YES does not depend on excusing the former flatness because this is a working product.

There is still room to refine the interface: the nested rectangular frames and repeated monospaced metadata give the lower chart controls a more mechanical rhythm than Fey's strongest surfaces, and the paper palette can look somewhat uniformly lilac. Those are remaining refinements, not the earlier decisive material gap. This re-review does not reassess the public portal's glass card or the material-lab specimen, which were not supplied as changed surfaces. No conclusion about motion or interaction follows from these stills.
