# NIVAR G2.1 — Weather, copper, paper

Generated source studies created 2026-09-11. Asset ownership only: no production React, backend, Alexandria, package or git edits in this pass.

The world is deliberately physical: territory carries energy; a copper connection makes transmission tangible; paper and vellum make examination visible. EV001 belongs to the native product composition, never to generated lettering, fabricated telemetry or an invented scientific instrument.

Original size 15,970,835 bytes. The 8 extra encoded source rows are cropped during a proportional normalization to the 16:9 desktop output.

Central fingers and vellum remain visible in portrait. This is a strong transition to interpretation, with native EV001 continuity layered above it.

101, Seedance 2.5 transmission: completed and preserved as the weather alternate. The closer tower and windblown foreground create storm atmosphere. It is darker, with less environmental range than the selected opening. Its long processing time triggered a targeted source-constrained study, 104, rather than a blind identical retry.

104, Kling v3.0 transmission locked: completed and selected as the recommended opening. It retains the curated image 01's centered tower, conductor convergence and layered hills. Grass, mist and camera perspective change naturally while the physical tower remains the anchor. The portrait crop retains the complete central tower, both conductor directions and a horizon break. Use `/g2/g21/transmission-locked-desktop.mp4` or `transmission-locked-mobile.mp4`, with matching `-poster.webp`. The source 01 was a 1k composition test, so the 1080p motion output should not be described as originating from 4K detail.

All 22 generations completed: 18 stills and four clips covering three concepts, with a deliberate second transmission implementation for comparison. The single first image was displayed by its original submission widget; the 21 batch-generated results were displayed together once after completion. `final-generation-results.json` preserves the final server parameters and results. `source-inspection.json` records source SHA-256, dimensions and full video decode checks. All four videos decoded without errors, and all five one-second frame transitions in each six-frame sample contained changing pixel content. Visual review—not that numeric check—established the preserved equipment topology and usable scene motion.

Optimized videos are exactly 6.000sec / 24fps, silent H.264 high profile, yuv420p, faststart. Desktop is 1600×900; portrait is 540×960 and centers the semantic action. Stills retain source proportions at a maximum edge of 1800px; portrait alternatives are 720×900. No final hero assembly is baked into these source films.

Live browser QA confirmed the optimized hydro and document streams with `readyState:4`, correct intrinsic dimensions, duration 6, no media error, and advancing `currentTime` from about 0.33sec to 5.5sec. The side-by-side captures in `references/motion-review-*-desktop-portrait.png` prove the retained composition. Reduced-motion behavior belongs to the consuming runtime and should show the matching poster instead of autoplaying a source clip.
