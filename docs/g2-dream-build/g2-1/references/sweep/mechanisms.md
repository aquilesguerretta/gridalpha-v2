## Access and interpretation limits

- All eight requested top-level sites loaded and have `*-landing.png` + JSON records. No sign-in wall blocked the selected references.
- Inspora and Recent were inspected as gallery media. Their credited X originals were not independently authenticated, downloaded, or treated as reusable NIVAR assets. The two-frame pairs prove visible changes, not interaction latency or complete motion timing.
- Waterworks' first location click selected/panned the retained map while its index stayed open; `water-detail.png` is that intermediate state. `water-detail-settled.png` is a separately opened, verified detail route. Do not describe the intermediate capture as the final detail UI.
- The OWID reference is its published redesign article and embedded demonstration, not a newly executed Grapher interaction.
- Carbon's first theme interaction capture (`carbon-dark-categorical.png`) was partially obscured by a late cookie banner. Use the subsequent explicit color-group capture instead: the banner remains below the fully visible palette, and selected state is verified. `interaction-observations.json` retains the initial failed In Common With button lookup; `final-checks.mjs` corrects it by clicking the actual checkbox label. The first Direct navigation attempt did not change route; the targeted footer click in `rechecks.mjs` did.
- Mobile means Chrome device emulation, not a physical touchscreen. Native pointer and keyboard input were used. No contact message, newsletter subscription, account change or purchase was submitted.
