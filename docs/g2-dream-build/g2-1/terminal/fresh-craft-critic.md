# Fresh craft verdict

**Does it compete at the reference bar? NO.**

Image-only review of `iteration-04-desktop.png`, `comparison-depth.png`, `dovetail-1440-0.png`, `dovetail-1440-700.png`, `linear-1440-0.png`, and `linear-1440-700.png`. No implementation or dossier was consulted. The comparison montage is supporting context; the full-size captures determine the verdict.

The Terminal has a credible identity: amber selection, geographic context, a substantial primary price, and the serif reading rail form a coherent energy product. Its density is appropriate. The remaining distance to the references is visible in the finish, rather than a need for additional widgets or marketing-scale whitespace.

**Single largest gap: the operational planes do not have enough material hierarchy.** Most of the map, chart, controls, and outer instrument share one dark field defined by fine rules. The brighter reading rail feels like an opaque rectangular sidebar. Dovetail's dashboard and raised context panel, and Linear's recessed navigation, working surface, and floating agent window, create clearer spatial relationships. The Terminal's widespread haze and texture soften those relationships instead of establishing them. Purposeful frosted glass is not legible in this capture.

1. **Establish three unmistakable graphite levels.** Keep the page frame darkest, lift the whole working instrument onto a restrained solid graphite surface, and give the reading context a modestly brighter raised surface. Use a controlled edge highlight and contact shadow at the actual elevation boundary. The map and chart should feel seated within this instrument, with fewer equally strong nested rectangles.
2. **Concentrate the glass effect where context overlays evidence.** A selected-region or selected-observation card can visibly sit over map or chart content, with a blurred trace of that content behind it, a quiet translucent tint, and one precise luminous edge. Remove the general atmospheric wash from the data area. The surface's location and transparency should explain its role immediately.
3. **Strengthen the small operational typography.** Region values, chart axes, timestamps, and controls currently become fragile against the textured dark field. Bring essential small labels toward 11–12 px at this desktop size, increase their contrast, and reduce the proliferation of faint monospaced captions and rules. Preserve the column structure and row density; improve readable information per glance.

Success would be a first glance that resolves the instrument's base, working surface, and selected context without inspecting borders, while the chart labels and regional values remain readily readable. This is a static visual verdict; the images cannot establish interaction or motion quality.
