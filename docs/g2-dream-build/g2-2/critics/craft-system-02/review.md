## 1. Software/Ariadne — NO

**The latest source composition has cleared its earlier problem, but the shared observation still visibly disappears during the phone handoff.**

The opening illustration, purple geography, copper thread, and serif observation are distinctive. The region→observation sequence has a coherent physical cause and effect. The latest source state now retains a compact map above the card on the phone, and the desktop source card fits fully inside the expanded scene. These are material improvements. The old clipping/map-obscuring captures remain only as superseded first-pass evidence; they are not the reason for this verdict.

The largest remaining gap is in the current source→Terminal transition. After clicking “Levar esta observação ao Terminal,” the carried 160,39 card becomes almost black before the final tooltip appears. In the sampled frame at approximately 1.14 seconds, the value near x96,y424 is barely legible against the chart; at approximately 1.45 seconds the destination card is fully opaque at x82–261,y367–478. The stable Terminal headline, 185,29, consequently becomes the only clearly readable large value during the seam. The intended observation can be followed logically, but not continuously by eye.

That is below the persistent-object standard visible in [CRED](https://60fps.design/shots/cred-credit-card-full-page-offers-swipe-interaction) and [Must](https://60fps.design/shots/must-movie-detail-sheet): the recognizable card/poster remains the anchor while its surroundings and scale change. Keep one legible observation card through the entire transfer, then exchange its shell/typography without an opacity trough. This is a small, specific motion correction, not a request to add spectacle.

## 2. Terminal public presentation and standalone — YES

The public presentation earns the pass as a focused, usable product scene. It presents one territory, one selected observation, a large uncluttered graph, a source disclosure, and a continuation that retains the selected place and instant. The desktop preview gives the graph room to breathe; the 390px version places territory controls, value, chart, source, and continuation in a clear sequence without squeezing in the full application.

The standalone instrument then adds the map, regional comparisons, working chart, reading panel, time windows, and source drawer with a consistent dark/amber hierarchy. Its large current value and the smaller selected-observation card have distinct jobs. The chart and the provenance drawer feel authored together. I inspected the region, instant, source, time-window, and metric changes in both full movies, plus the current live screen at both widths.

[Linear](https://linear.app/) and [Origin](https://www.useorigin.com/) are stronger in some individual presentation effects, but the relevant category principle is already present here: choose an intelligible product slice, show it at a useful scale, and expand into the instrument deliberately. NIVAR does this with its own visual identity. I am not withholding a pass for lacking a decorative phone frame or unrelated cinematic effects.

## 3. Diógenes finale — NO

The engraved figure and lantern are strong assets, the negative space is calm, and the source/method interactions are thoughtfully contained. The limitation is the actual ending composition after normal scroll.

At the desktop end position, the closing heading has a DOM box y37.7–180.0 while the sticky header occupies y0–84. Roughly 46px of its top is covered. On the phone, the heading is y−25.0–60.2 and the header ends at y68: the entire closing heading has disappeared by the time the final invitation, brand sign-off, and footer are visible. This is reproduced in 14-finale-desktop-rest.png and 19-finale-phone-rest.png, and also appears in the supplied finale movies. The reviewed finale is the version captured before the separately announced pending correction.

The largest gap versus [Podium’s actual close](https://podium.global/) is the missing composed final resting frame. Podium keeps its large object and invitation together as the page ends. NIVAR currently asks the viewer to remember the closing headline from the preceding scroll position. Reallocate roughly 47px of vertical budget on desktop and 93px on phone, or otherwise compose the end state so the heading, lantern, and invitation coexist below the navigation. Preserve the quiet tone; this does not require copying Podium’s 3D style.
