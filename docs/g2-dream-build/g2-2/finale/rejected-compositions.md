# Rejected alternatives and observed iterations

## Composition decision before product work

Three different directions were considered in the independent research. Only A was selected for implementation. B and C were written spatial studies; they were not rendered or approved alternatives.

- **A · Open working folio — selected.** One oversized Diógenes gesture addresses one native evidence record. The five methods remain attached to that record. Fits the light mineral territory and keeps provenance immediately readable.
- **B · Question crosses a threshold — rejected.** A dark operational path would cross into a light boundary with Diógenes standing between them. More spatial drama, but risked suggesting dark operational work is ignorance and light is truth, and would introduce a second portal metaphor.
- **C · House palimpsest — rejected.** Five patron fragments would emerge around Diógenes in one layered engraving. Strong family presence, but high risk of a Greek mural or a logo constellation that obscured the evidence.

Full spatial descriptions and observed live reference evidence remain in `../family-finale-research/observations-and-compositions.md`.

## Remaining question for the critic

The ending now has a dominant figure and an examinable record. Its largest residual risk is that the five methods still depend on concise labels and changing emphasis, while Diógenes's bodily gesture remains a composed illustration. Judge whether the action and shared record are enough to make it a house synthesis, or whether it needs a stronger causal motion beat. Do not convert that open craft question into a PASS based on checklists.
