## Asset direction

One generated charcoal/lavender intaglio on mineral paper, with the figure right and empty left space. The reference was NIVAR's existing Diógenes engraving. The new arm/lantern geometry was required because enlarging the previous square specimen could not produce the intended relation to the folio. The illustration carries a philosophical attribute; the data and method are native content, not baked into the artwork. No invented scientific instrument is depicted.

## Motion capture disclosure

An initial Chrome screencast omitted late desktop frames despite successful native interactions. It was not accepted as final evidence. Both final recordings use a timed loop of real browser screenshots while native wheel and pointer input executes, with timestamps retained for encoding. The final desktop contact sheet includes approach, open note, closed note, selected Questionar and resolution. This recording establishes sequence and behavior; the live page remains the right place to judge high-frame-rate smoothness.
