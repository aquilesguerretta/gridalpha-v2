# Real Brazil — G2.2 source desk

Research completed 2026-09-11. These are research originals, not a runtime asset directory. Five original JPEGs were downloaded from the original-file links on the primary Commons pages, inspected visually, and hashed. Nothing was generated, color graded, cropped, or installed into the application by this research task.

The strongest immediate choice is **aerial + power lines + control room**. Each has a clear attribution-only license. Their photographic content already supplies three distinct scales: territory, infrastructure, human operation. Native interface construction should provide the transformation. Moving a camera across a photograph is still photographic animation, not recovered documentary footage.

## What changed after inspecting the originals

1. **The pack's spillway is dry.** The title is correct but the suggested “hydro flow” role is misleading. The open concrete ramps and wet staining give tremendous physical scale and a precise vanishing point; there is no depicted discharge to animate or measure.
2. **The power-line photograph depicts pylons on the Paraguay side of Itaipu.** Use “Itaipu · infraestrutura binacional” / “margem paraguaia.” Do not call it a Brazilian-side transmission corridor.
3. **The generator hall is an extreme horizontal panorama with strong green lighting.** It is an intriguing industrial image, but is weaker for mobile, requires an intentional color treatment, and the source includes an inconsistent author credit request. It is not a first-choice hero asset.
4. **Itaipu_virtual is a report about a 2019 science/technology exhibition.** Its filename does not establish clean infrastructure B-roll. The Commons page also retains its explicit human-review warning. It is not approved as a production source here.

## Concrete scene proposals

### A. Territory becomes a source record — preferred opening

Use [the aerial original](originals/itaipu-aerial.jpg) as an uninterrupted physical field before any major interface. The diagonal dam begins near the lower left and recedes toward the upper right; retain that direction into the next transition. A slow digital descent toward the powerhouse is legitimate editorial movement of a still. Start with the truthful caption **“Itaipu · rio Paraná · fotografia de 2013”**, with full credit reachable nearby.

On desktop, a wide crop centered around the powerhouse and downstream river makes the dam rather than empty reservoir the subject. On mobile, the vertical original can remain nearly whole; shift the attention down its real diagonal instead of cropping to an anonymous patch of water. Do not invent new parallax disocclusions or machine-generated unseen geography. Any Higgsfield transformation based on this source would need to be labeled generated, losing its role as purely documentary origin.

The first persistent object should be a **source anchor**, not a fictitious MW measurement attributed to Itaipu. A marked location can become the photograph's dated source tab; a clearly separate, visibly identified demonstrative record can then teach the method. Avoid a wire running from a turbine to the existing 68 MW fixture, which would falsely imply measured production.

### B. Repetition becomes structure — preferred organization passage

[The three towers](originals/itaipu-power-lines.jpg) already create a powerful receding sequence. Keep all three in desktop framing, with the nearest tower at the right; mobile should frame the middle/nearest tower deliberately, rather than shrinking three towers into noise. The authored movement can follow the existing overhead conductors until one *documented source marker* locks, then carry that marker into a native record. Its visual rhythm can become a sequence of aligned rows, but the conductors must not pretend to be a measured chart series.

Use restrained desaturation only if needed. The actual blue sky is physical color, not a reason to introduce a blue product palette. Credit: Leandro Neumann Ciuffo; CC BY 2.0. The image establishes **binational context**, not NIVAR hardware or an NIVAR installation.

### C. Operational space becomes navigable context — preferred Ariadne bridge

[The control room](originals/itaipu-control-room.jpg) gives real desks, operators, a diagram wall, depth, and honest materiality. A wide upper/middle crop keeps the room's curved geometry and excludes the large foreground floor. Keep the Itaipu name visible only as documentary identification, never as a customer or endorsement logo. It should introduce the act of organizing rather than advertise that the pictured staff use NIVAR.

Build native diagram/source labels in the foreground while the historical photograph retreats. Do not replace its screen contents with fictional live readings. Caption **“Sala de Controle Central de Itaipu · registro de 2010”** distinguishes historical source from the present-day NIVAR product.

### D. Empty ramp as an examination of absence — optional alternative

[The spillway](originals/itaipu-spillway.jpg) can be unusually appropriate to Sócrates: an apparently powerful system shown without the expected flow. Keep it a physical scene, not evidence of a drought or an operational conclusion (neither follows from this photo alone). The central divider can become a deliberate compositional split between claim and missing evidence. If adapted, release the adapted photograph under CC BY-SA 4.0 and retain a downloadable attributed asset. Do not use it merely as a watery backdrop; it contains no spilling water.

### E. Generator hall — reserve, not selected

[The panoramic hall](originals/itaipu-generator-hall.jpg) is strongest as a narrow architectural pause, not a full-viewport portrait crop. The generator cover gives a recognizable physical datum, but speculative identification of a unit or reading is prohibited. Its license notice formally says BY 3.0 while the author's requested adjacent credit says BY-SA-3.0. This discrepancy is recorded below; no runtime derivative was made.

## Real motion search — no high-quality cleared source selected

The search was expanded beyond the pack. It did not yield a high-quality real Brazilian infrastructure clip with sufficiently clear rights for immediate runtime use.

- [Itaipu virtual](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Itaipu_virtual.webm): TV BrasilGov, CC BY 3.0 recorded by an automated YouTube check. The page explicitly says human review must still evaluate other copyright issues. It remains **reference only**. No binary downloaded.
- [Foz do Iguaçu, ITAIPU, Brazil](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Foz_do_Igua%C3%A7u,_ITAIPU,_Brazil.webm): Rillke, own work, filmed from a bus in 2014, 640×480. FAL / GFDL 1.2 alternatives and required credit are shown. Even before motion evaluation, this resolution is unsuitable for the proposed large hero; no binary downloaded or production clearance asserted.
- [Itaipu video category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Videos_about_Itaipu_Dam): mostly TV BrasilGov/NBR political or news reports. A Commons/YouTube license flag does not clear every third-party shot, music track, or piece of embedded material. No reports were silently turned into NIVAR B-roll.
- [Official Itaipu media bank](https://bancodeimagens.itaipu.gov.br/fotoweb/) returned 502 during verification. No current production permission established.
- [Furnas archive URL](https://www.furnas.com.br/subsecao/248/fotos?culture=pt) redirected to AXIA's home page, whose footer states all rights reserved. The promised historical archive was not available at the supplied URL. No AXIA photography downloaded.

**Recommended immediate strategy:** truthful real photography + authored native camera/composition + native evidence transformations + separately disclosed generated interstitial motion where it creates a transition. Do not advertise a digital pan of a still as “real footage.” This recommendation does not claim the hero's motion bar is satisfied merely by adding these assets.

## EPE geographic study

The [EPE Webmap page](https://www.epe.gov.br/pt/publicacoes-dados-abertos/publicacoes/webmap-epe) states CC BY 4.0 and describes vector/raster downloads, WMS, and layer metadata. The [existing-system service](https://gisepeprd2.epe.gov.br/arcgis/rest/services/SMA/Webmap_EPE_Sistema_Eletrico_Existente/MapServer) uses SIRGAS 2000 / EPSG:4674 and supports GeoJSON queries.

Layer [2 (UHE)](https://gisepeprd2.epe.gov.br/arcgis/rest/services/SMA/Webmap_EPE_Sistema_Eletrico_Existente/MapServer/2) provides plant names and geographic points; layer [9 (transmission)](https://gisepeprd2.epe.gov.br/arcgis/rest/services/SMA/Webmap_EPE_Sistema_Eletrico_Existente/MapServer/9) provides real named polylines. Layer [10 (SIN subsystems)](https://gisepeprd2.epe.gov.br/arcgis/rest/services/SMA/Webmap_EPE_Sistema_Eletrico_Existente/MapServer/10) carries explicit **EPE, ONS, IBGE** credit and a 2020 creation date. Its polygons are state/subsystem context, not proof of a real-time operational boundary or a live-data integration.

The individual metadata XML was inspected and saved under `source-pages/`: UHE/transmission metadata have empty credit/abstract fields; subsystem metadata repeats the three organizations. No geometry was downloaded or wired into product logic. Use this for a later dated contextual map only after retaining layer provenance and checking its intended geographic meaning. Existing protected sample geography should not be overwritten for an art experiment.
