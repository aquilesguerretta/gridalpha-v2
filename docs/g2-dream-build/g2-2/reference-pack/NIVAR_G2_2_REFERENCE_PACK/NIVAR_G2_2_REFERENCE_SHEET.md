# NIVAR G2.2 - Reference Sheet for the next craft pass

## 0. What this sheet is for

This is not a moodboard and not a list of fashionable websites.

It is a **mechanism library** for attacking the remaining G2.1 weaknesses with external evidence.

The operating rule is:

> **Borrow mechanisms. Do not clone copyrighted layouts, imagery, wordmarks, or distinctive expression. Reuse code/assets only where the license clearly allows it.**

For every external reference Codex uses, it should answer:

1. What exact mechanism is stronger than NIVAR today?
2. What problem does that mechanism solve?
3. What is the smallest NIVAR-native translation of that mechanism?
4. How will we compare the new render to the reference?
5. What part must *not* be copied?

The next craft pass should be judged against live references continuously, not against the previous NIVAR version.

---

# 1. The five weaknesses to attack first

## 1A. Hero film

Current weakness:

- strong six-verb concept
- footage and overlays improved in G2.1
- interface still tends to arrive fully built
- orange connector line is sometimes semantically empty
- not enough persistent-object continuity
- insufficient family presence
- insufficient real Brazilian specificity
- motion feels like edited chapters more than one causal cinematic system

Target:

> The user should watch reality become evidence, evidence become structure, structure become a signal, the signal become a question, the question survive contradiction, and the result become transferable knowledge.

Hard rules for the next hero:

- no connector line unless both endpoints are visible and semantically exact
- no major panel should simply spawn fully assembled
- a persistent evidence identity should survive at least four state transitions
- use real Brazilian energy imagery wherever it materially strengthens truth
- family verbs must be felt in motion, not merely listed below the film
- mobile gets its own framing and pacing
- end should hand off naturally into the House narrative rather than simply loop

## 1B. Software / Ariadne

Current weakness:

- Terminal is exposed as a product block rather than *revealed as a system*
- Ariadne's micro symbol is weak
- Software has less emotional power than Hardware or Advisory

Target:

> Ariadne does not show a dashboard. Ariadne turns a selected piece of complexity into a navigable path.

Desired sequence:

region / datum -> relationship -> chart -> source -> interpretation -> full Terminal

The full Terminal should be earned through motion or interaction.

## 1C. Family identity

Current weakness:

- large intaglio figures are promising
- utility SVGs are too weak to serve as family identity
- family pages still share too much of the same editorial grammar

Target:

- utility mark may remain only as a small functional cue
- emotional identity comes from character, gesture, object, scale, material, and motion
- each family gets a behavioral motion grammar

## 1D. Diógenes / closing

Current weakness:

- Diógenes is cropped and visually secondary
- ending does not gather the house
- family system disappears at the exact moment the experience should synthesize it

Target:

> The close should make the entire house intelligible in one memorable act.

Diógenes should reveal or gather:

Hefesto / medir
Ariadne / organizar
Argos / observar
Sócrates / questionar
Perseu / transmitir
Diógenes / procurar

## 1E. Terminal desire and motion

Current weakness:

- structure and material hierarchy are substantially better
- still screenshots now compete better
- motion and interactive delight are behind the visual finish

Target:

- selection propagates across map, chart, reading, source, and time
- graph states morph rather than hard-switch
- source/probe overlays feel lifted and materially distinct
- map remains spatial context, not decoration
- Terminal feels like a living instrument without implying fake live data

## 2.15 Codrops: ZERO

URL:
https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/17/zero-the-engineering-behind-a-defiant-interactive-narrative/

Mechanism:
A six-stage scroll narrative is built from self-contained segments. Each segment owns enter, scrub, update and teardown behavior, while one master progress value coordinates the whole experience. The project also uses explicit interactive gates rather than treating every transition as passive scroll.

Why NIVAR should care:
This is a stronger technical model for the six NIVAR verbs than six separate scene components that merely crossfade. Each verb can own a clear local lifecycle while handing one persistent evidence state to the next.

Translate:
- one master method progress value
- one segment per verb
- preload only the next necessary media
- handoff EV-001 between segment lifecycles
- reserve interaction gates for moments where user intent actually matters

Do not copy:
The frost, glass shatter, tunnel, or anti-university narrative.

## 2.16 Codrops: Podium

URL:
https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/23/podium-building-a-website-where-running-becomes-storytelling/

Mechanism:
The site is treated as a continuous sequence instead of a page stack. GSAP/ScrollTrigger, Lenis, React state, and Three.js share a common rhythm. The authors explicitly emphasize restraint and directional continuity over a large number of effects.

Translate:
Portal chapters should emerge from the previous chapter rather than reset. Motion direction can become part of NIVAR's reading rhythm.

Most important lesson:
More motion is not the target. Better temporal composition is.

## 2.17 Codrops: Ten Years Away

URL:
https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/08/ten-years-away-designing-an-interactive-comic-for-studio375s-tenth-anniversary/

Mechanism:
A chapter does not appear as one finished page. Individual visual panels arrive separately, move into alignment, and only then become the complete composition.

Translate:
This is almost exactly the corrective mechanism requested for NIVAR dashboards: the instrument should assemble from meaningful parts before it becomes a complete panel.

Possible NIVAR use:
- chart axis arrives
- sample values arrive
- missing intervals reserve space
- source/period/unit lock into place
- interpretation plane appears last

Do not copy:
Comic styling, halftone language, or horizontal chapter framing.

# 15. Behavioral identity proposal for the six patrons

These are testable mechanisms, not approved final art.

## Hefesto / MEDIR

Motion grammar:
- measure
- lock
- calibrate
- align
- tighten

Visual behavior:
A measurement jaw, datum, boundary, or grid moves into precise alignment.

Avoid:
industrial stock montage with no measurement semantics.

## Ariadne / ORGANIZAR

Motion grammar:
- thread persists
- relationships connect
- selections propagate
- source remains reachable

Visual behavior:
One continuous path survives between stages and becomes interaction, not decoration.

Avoid:
generic maze or squiggly SVG used as a logo wallpaper.

## Argos / OBSERVAR

Motion grammar:
- focus
- compare
- reveal change
- maintain horizon

Visual behavior:
The observation field changes while frame of reference remains stable.

Avoid:
generic radar sweep.

## Sócrates / QUESTIONAR

Motion grammar:
- proposition enters
- opposing evidence enters
- gap remains visible
- conclusion refuses to close early

Visual behavior:
Two planes or claims face each other without fake resolution.

Avoid:
red/green pass/fail theater.

## Perseu / TRANSMITIR

Motion grammar:
- knowledge moves forward
- one object becomes teachable structure
- progress accumulates

Visual behavior:
A reading / diagram / concept transfers across stages.

Avoid:
flame looping as a decorative celebration.

## Diógenes / PROCURAR

Motion grammar:
- reveal what was obscured
- expose source/relationship
- final answer stays open to further evidence

Visual behavior:
Lantern/light is used only to reveal actual hidden relationships, labels, or family fragments.

Avoid:
generic glow or spotlight with nothing to discover.

# 16. Proposed Portal narrative bar

A stronger next Portal should roughly achieve this emotional curve:

## OPEN

Real Brazilian energy territory. Minimal thesis. Hero film begins with physical reality.

## EVIDENCE

EV-001 builds itself from measurement, gaps, source, period, and unit.

## HOUSE

The persistent object becomes the House navigation. Family verbs appear as behaviors, not a list of decorative icons.

## ARGOS

Observation/publication chapter.

## SÓCRATES

Claim/evidence tension.

## PERSEU

Human knowledge / learning / transfer.

## ARIADNE

The light public system transforms into operational product. Terminal builds from relationships.

## HEFESTO

Physical field and measurement close the loop back to reality.

## DIÓGENES

The house reassembles. All five capabilities are visible as one system. Search remains open.

This is not a mandated section order. It is the bar for narrative coherence.

# 17. Visual pass criteria for G2.2

## Hero

PASS only if:

- real motion material is visible
- persistent evidence identity is obvious without labels
- at least one major UI panel builds progressively from semantic parts
- no decorative connector line exists
- Brazilian reality is clearly identifiable in multiple moments
- family behaviors are present in the motion language
- mobile is composed separately
- final transition naturally enters the House

## Software

PASS only if:

- the full Terminal is not merely dumped into the opening
- Ariadne's relationship/path behavior is visible
- at least one interaction/scroll state visibly creates the product from partial information
- light-to-dark transition feels intentional
- full product appears as consequence, not illustration

## Family system

PASS only if:

- removing family names still leaves noticeably different behavioral rhythms
- utility glyphs are not doing the emotional brand work
- hero patron art has real scale
- no family becomes Greek cosplay

## Diógenes close

PASS only if:

- Diógenes is deliberate, not accidentally cropped
- the close visually synthesizes the house
- all family verbs or presences are recoverable
- CTA belongs to the narrative
- final frame is memorable at thumbnail scale

## Terminal

PASS only if:

- selection propagates visibly
- graph states morph when context changes
- source/probe material layer is clearly elevated
- geography/time/source remain connected
- motion never implies data is live when it is synthetic
- still frame remains excellent without motion

# 18. What should *not* be copied

Do not clone:

- Linear purple/black brand language
- Fey financial terminal language
- Dovetail brand colors or product hierarchy wholesale
- NASA worm, meatball, patches, or typographic identity
- Rivian photography/compositions
- Media.Work art direction as a skin
- any Supahero screenshot or source asset
- any Recent/Inspora artwork
- any 60fps product styling

Copy:

- shared-element continuity
- scale relationships
- progress models
- panel construction logic
- scroll choreography
- material hierarchy
- role-based typography
- character architecture principles
- source/provenance proximity
- selection propagation
- real-world imagery scale
