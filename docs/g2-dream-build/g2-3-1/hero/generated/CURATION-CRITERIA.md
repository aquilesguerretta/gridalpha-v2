These inserts are symbolic material cinema, not a depiction of Itaipu, an operating measurement device, or any real energy record. The real Brazilian infrastructure source and the illustrative dataset must retain independent provenance in the Hero.

## Candidate A — calibration contact

The engraved copper rail and steel jaw let Hefesto's measurement become visible as a physical action. The single jaw contact should be legible before the evidence interface enters. A restrained lateral dolly introduces real parallax and moving specular reflections. The upper-left paper field is deliberately quiet, but the preferred editorial treatment is a cut into an evidence sheet after contact, not continuously placing dense text over the contact.

Reject if: the jaw passes through metal; calibrations become hallucinated numbers; sparks/fire make the gesture theatrical; the camera only performs a still-image zoom; focus never reveals the contact; generated text appears. Reject orange-heavy decorative luxury-product imagery that loses a readable measurement action.

## Candidate B — optical carrier

The glass-to-paper projection expresses observation and transmission. Focusing a projected trace makes evidence become readable without pretending that an animated beam is energy data. The conceptual path is image → optical evidence → precise native interface. The glass carrier remains a distinct physical object; no fantastical hologram is acceptable.

Reject if: traces multiply without physical cause; projected marks behave like magic light; glass warps; the paper changes shape as a liquid; the take is merely static; pseudo-scientific text appears. The filmed trace must never be used as the source of the synthetic analytical values.
