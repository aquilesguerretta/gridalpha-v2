# Exploração formal com Image Gen

Ferramenta: `image_gen.imagegen`, modo integrado, duas gerações novas em 13 setembro de 2026. Nenhuma imagem de referência, nenhum logo externo como entrada. Os resultados são estudos de desenho; não são mestres vetoriais, evidência documental ou prova de tamanho real.

## Prancha 01 — cinco famílias

Arquivo `generated-exploration-five.png`, origem `exec-a579ece2-6858-4a84-bb90-9e26ff2afd2e.png` na pasta de imagens geradas do Codex.

Prompt: “Use case: logo-brand. Asset: one serious art-direction contact sheet of FIVE original compact institutional family insignias for NIVAR, a Brazilian energy intelligence house. Not letters, not monograms, not UI icons. White/off-white sheet, five horizontal rows; each row shows one authored one-color dark ink mark large, then 24px and 16px small proofs. Small discreet row labels only: ARGOS, SOCRATES, PERSEU, ARIADNE, HEFESTO. Aim for persistent institutional printer's marks with confident dense silhouettes, asymmetric optical drawing, deliberate negative space, humane curved shoulders, decisive cuts. Think original scientific institute imprints, industrial foundry signatures, research archives, classical modern publishing—but copy no existing logo. FIVE DIFFERENT TOPOLOGIES, same tactile ink/mass/cut grammar. ARGOS observes: observed field and a fixed witness, not an eye, lens, radar, aperture-logo or letter G. SOCRATES questions: unequal opposing premises, one interrupts an otherwise plausible closure; an open conclusion, not an S, question mark, quotation marks, medical cross, symmetric brackets or yin-yang. PERSEU transmits: the exact fragment absent from a source mass is visibly carried forward, not a P, rr, RSS, arrow or wing. ARIADNE organizes: distinct unequal entries coming from different orientations physically converge into a shared datum, not E, psi, trident, checkmark, thread squiggle or network icon. HEFESTO measures/builds: material registration of a reference standard and a receiving gauge, not hammer/flame/anvil or letter H. Essential: no alphabet-like set; no five variants of circle or square; no seals with borders; no fine linework; no Greek portrait; no generic geometric technology startup feel; no gradient, bevel or 3D. These should feel like authored insignias one might stamp on an institutional field notebook, useful at 16px, not decorative illustration. Each mark should contain few strong masses and a memorable negative-space relationship.”

Decisão: a topologia de oposição desigual de Sócrates rompeu a leitura do S. Campo/horizonte/testemunha de Argos e matriz/fragmento de Perseu forneceram caminhos novos. A junção radial de Ariadne foi rejeitada por associação genérica; o novo Hefesto gerado foi rejeitado em favor do esquadro/padrão vetorial, mais claro.

## Prancha 02 — Ariadne

Arquivo `generated-exploration-ariadne.png`, origem `exec-15af08d1-2bec-4fdf-8c81-b4624b7a4c3a.png`.

Prompt: “Use case logo-brand. ONE white presentation sheet with THREE highly authored original one-color dark ink institutional insignia concepts for ARIADNE, the software family of NIVAR, a serious Brazilian energy intelligence house. This is a compact printer's mark, a repeatable 16–32px family identifier, not a UI icon. Theme: ORGANIZE — unequal entries or fragments physically resolve into a common readable register, preserving their differences. Each candidate must show a different memorable solid silhouette and convincing relationship of mass/cut/negative space. Broad sculptural ink masses, humane concave and convex shoulders, precise irregular cuts, perhaps the force of historic scientific publisher imprints or atelier signatures. Not techno-futurism. Do NOT depict letters/initials/monograms, E, W, psi, trident, pitchfork, checkmark, node network, thread, maze, generic AI startup three-way join, sparkles, flower, infinity knot, three identical radial arms, gear, book outline, or conventional app icons. Avoid many detached small bits. The source behavior should be visible in how the parts truly meet, not require a caption to explain it. Think an original imprint that could be incised into a field instrument or pressed in a library folio. Three DIFFERENT concepts, no five variants of a square. Present each at roughly 180px wide and alongside small actual-looking ink proofs. No 3D, material texture, perspective, border/seal, gradient, shadows or fine lines. Only labels 01, 02, 03.”

Decisão: a primeira rota sugeriu entradas em canais que resolvem numa margem. A releitura nativa une de fato esses canais numa espinha, abre seus intervalos para 16 px e elimina a textura da geração. As outras duas rotas (lamelas verticais e recipientes empilhados) foram rejeitadas por diferença de gramática e parentesco com ícones comuns.

## Da imagem ao master

Os resultados gerados não foram simplesmente vetorizados por limiar. Foram redesenhados em paths novos, com decisões de escala, intervalo e topologia. `topology-revision-03.json` documenta esses contornos e `topology-revision-03.png` apresenta a rasterização REAL a 16/24/32 px. As miniaturas imaginadas pela geração não contam como teste técnico.
