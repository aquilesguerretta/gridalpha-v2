## Material study status

The first Blender comparison uses exact candidate paths on a continuous mineral/graphite surface. It revealed an overlit, too smooth treatment and positive relief. Those six renders remain rejected process evidence.

The selected-only correction is complete and was visually inspected in both modes: `blender/selected-interval-mineral.png` and `blender/selected-interval-graphite.png`. The exact glyphs are physically subtracted 0.24 mm into the surface; pigment lies below the surface and the cut-edge radius is 12 microns. This is a continuous material field, not a plaque or an invented laboratory object. The graphite now remains dark under restrained raking light. The editable new scene is `blender/nivar-g23-selected-incision.blend`, generated by `blender/selected-inscription.py` with Blender 5.2.1 LTS in a separate background process.

Review the before/after in `material-study.html`.
