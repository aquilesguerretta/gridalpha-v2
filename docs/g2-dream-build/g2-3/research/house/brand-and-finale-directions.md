# G2.3 · Grounded house directions

Recommendations, not owner approvals. These follow the inspected G2/G2.1/G2.2 record and G2.3 owner constraints.

## One house, three scales

1. **Institutional scale:** NIVAR wordmark, house descriptor, stable rail, explicit current family, real search/account/theme. This signature remains dominant even inside Terminal. Product and family names never become substitute parent brands.
2. **Editorial scale:** existing large patron engravings, cropped to their meaningful gesture, embedded in the same paper/graphite/copper world as native evidence or media. A character is part of the composition's structure.
3. **Utility scale:** ordinary text, ordered family numbers and actual UI affordances. Remove existing micro/standard emotional SVGs. A small index does not need a new eye/flame/anvil/thread logo.

Do not shrink the rich patron images to 16px and call that the new symbol system. At that size the family name/index is the reliable identifier; the master NIVAR remains the mark.

## Header mechanism

A stable master rail should gain authority through brand proportion and material, with a secondary current-context line that transforms as the page is entered. On Portal it can identify the house and active chapter; on a family it names family/patron/verb in type; on Terminal it becomes a compact working context. A small scroll response can tighten descriptor spacing and increase the material's opacity while preserving navigation geometry. Avoid bouncing height changes that disturb reading.

Mobile should use a deliberately composed house index with large readable family rows, short behavior descriptions and real direct product entries. Keep account, search and mode discoverable. Provide current-state semantics and focus management; do not rely on a moving underline as the only active indicator.

## Replace the finale's weak method interaction

Keep Diógenes, lantern direction, question and final action. Replace the default tiny static graph with a useful **examination of one assertion**:

- Begin with the assertion: “De 68 a 108 MW: isso demonstra uma tendência?”
- Let users inspect the actual six timestamp slots; two remain visibly absent.
- Selecting **Medir** opens the exact observation, unit and synthetic origin; **Organizar** aligns the six timestamps; **Observar** shows four measured values and the two breaks; **Questionar** puts endpoint change beside coverage/unknown path; **Transmitir** produces an inspectable short reading retaining both limits.
- The selected action changes the presented evidence, not only the heading or border. Native source/detail access remains reachable by keyboard.
- Diógenes returns the useful unresolved question: what measurements would change this interpretation? It must not fabricate those missing values or treat an illustrative series as an operational recommendation.

The conceptual shift is from a five-logo method diagram to five operations on the same evidence. An expanded native notebook can provide detail; the visible finale should remain a strong emotional close. Mobile presents one selected operation with clear previous/next or tab controls, not five narrow columns.

## Largest risks to carry into fresh review

- A heavier header with unchanged brand drawing may still feel neutral; evaluate actual proportions and silhouette.
- Replacing glyphs with numbered labels solves weak utility branding but does not by itself create family identity; the patron compositions must carry it.
- New motion can obscure the same evidence in the hero. Preserve the existing keyed samples and explicit source caveats through every intermediate frame.
- Night mode requires all finale/material/dialog states, not one background override.
- More footage can dilute factual Brazil. Historic photography, generated connective material and synthetic product data must remain explicitly distinct.
